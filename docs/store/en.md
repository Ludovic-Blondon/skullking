## Description (4000)

Skull Scores keeps score for your Skull King games, from the first trick to the podium.

Pen and paper did the job — until someone bids zero in round 9, captures the Skull King with a
mermaid and claims their +40. This app knows the scoring by heart, adds it up for you, and lets
the table get on with playing.

THE WHOLE SCORING SHEET
• Classic scoring: 20 points per trick bid, ±10 per card on a zero bid
• Rascal the Gambler variant, with the round potential and the cannonball
• Capture bonuses: coloured 14s, mermaid over Skull King, Skull King over pirates
• Advanced cards: Kraken, White Whale, and Loot with its alliances
• Pirate abilities: Harry the Giant (bid ±1) and Rascal's bet (±10 / ±20)
• 2021 edition and older ones: the scoring follows the box you brought out

BUILT FOR A REAL TABLE
• Two-step entry — bids first, then tricks, the way the round is played
• The sums check themselves: an overbid table shows up straight away
• A mistyped round can be corrected, and every later total recomputes
• The screen stays awake during a game
• A dark mode made for a table at night

AFTER THE GAME
• The full history of your game nights, each with its score sheet
• End-of-game honours: the Visionary, the Daredevil, the Admiral of Zero
• Per-player stats — average score, exact bids, zero bids held
• All-time standings ranked by average score, not by total

ASKING FOR NOTHING
• Fully offline: no connection needed, ever
• No account, no ads, no trackers, no data collected
• Your data stays on your device, and exports to a file whenever you want
• English, French, Spanish, German

app is original.
